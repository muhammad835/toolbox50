What stays on your device?

Uploaded images and text are processed in your browser. Notes, tasks, flashcards, plans, and budgets save to this browser only. Clearing browser data clears those saves, so export important work.

How translation works

AI translation uses your browser's on-device Translator when supported. It may first download language models. The optional Google Translate button opens an external service and sends only the text you choose to open there.

Good to know

Results are useful starting points. Review translations and calculations before relying on them. Toolbox contains no analytics, ads, sign-ups, or paid API keys.
